# Project Instructions

## Cat Mascot Image Generation

- Before generating, editing, or requesting any cat mascot image, read `docs/cat-mascot-design-spec.md`.
- Treat that document as the source of truth for the cat's proportions, outline weight, eye weight, palette, simplicity, prompt constraints, and validation checks.
- Preserve the established mascot across variants. Do not introduce a new style, palette, body proportion, or level of detail without explicitly updating the design specification first.
- After generation, check the output against the specification, especially transparent background, silhouette clarity at mobile size, outline thickness, eye thickness, ear proportion, and simplified mouth and paws.
